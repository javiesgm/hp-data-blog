## What is it?

A place for GBIF staff and guest bloggers to contribute:

* Statistics
* Graphs
* Tutorials
* Ideas
* Opinions

## Who can contribute?

If you would like to contribute, contact [jwaller@gbif.org](mailto:jwaller@gbif.org). **Guest posts are very welcome.**

## How can I write a post?

There is a short tutorial, [How to write a post](https://github.com/gbif/data-blog#readme), in the blog's GitHub repository.

## What about the developer blog?

The [developer blog](http://gbif.blogspot.com/) is no longer updated, but its archive is kept online.

## Disclaimer

All posts represent the ideas of the author. No posts on this blog are to be considered a GBIF or Institutional policy.
